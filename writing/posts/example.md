# Biotech and the long view

<p class="writing-post-meta">May 2026</p>

Biotech rewards people who can hold two timelines in their head at once: the quarter-to-quarter reality of programs and partnerships, and the decade-long arc of a platform truly maturing.

## Compound learning

The most useful skill I have tried to cultivate is not prediction—it is *iteration with memory*. Small experiments, documented clearly, stack into judgment faster than hot takes ever will.

## What I watch for

When hype cycles compress timelines in the headlines, I look for teams that still invest in boring excellence: assay quality, cohort design, manufacturing discipline, and the unglamorous work of turning a signal into a repeatable process.
